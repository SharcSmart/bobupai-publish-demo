# iX3 vs the alternatives

## Tesla Model Y

The obvious cross-shop. The Model Y wins on charging network maturity and software,
and usually on outright efficiency. The iX3 wins on interior materials, ride quality
and how quiet it is at motorway speed. Which matters more depends almost entirely on
how much long-distance driving you do.

## Audi Q4 e-tron

The closest match on character — another premium mid-size SUV built to feel
conventional. Interior space is comparable. The decision usually comes down to dealer
proximity and which interior you prefer.

## Volvo EX40

Smaller inside and priced below both. If the iX3's practicality is more than you
need, the EX40 is the more sensible purchase, and it is the one to drive before
committing to a larger car.

## Choosing between them

| Priority | Likely pick |
|---|---|
| Long motorway trips, frequent rapid charging | Tesla Model Y |
| Quiet, conventional premium SUV | BMW iX3 or Audi Q4 e-tron |
| Smallest sensible footprint | Volvo EX40 |
