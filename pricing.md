# iX3 pricing — list price and what changes it

## How the list price is structured

Like most premium SUVs, the headline figure is a starting point rather than a
transaction price. Trim level, wheels, paint and driver-assistance packages all move
it, and they move it more than buyers expect.

## The options that matter most

### Wheels
Larger wheels look better and cost range. They are also one of the pricier options.
This is the trade-off most owners say they would reconsider.

### Paint
Metallic and special finishes carry a meaningful premium and are the easiest place to
save money without affecting how the car drives.

### Driver assistance
Worth having if you do long motorway journeys; hard to justify if you mostly drive in
town.

## Running costs

Electricity at home is the dominant variable and, charged overnight on an
EV-friendly tariff, is where most of the saving against a combustion equivalent comes
from. Rapid charging on the road costs several times as much per kilometre. Servicing
is generally lighter than a combustion equivalent — no oil, no exhaust, and brake
wear reduced by regeneration.
