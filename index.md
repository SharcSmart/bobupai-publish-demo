# BMW iX3 — an independent guide

The iX3 is a mid-size all-electric SUV. It shares its silhouette with the combustion
X3, which is the whole point: it is meant to feel ordinary to drive and ordinary to
live with, rather than announce itself as an electric car.

## Who it suits

It fits people replacing a mid-size premium SUV who want the same interior space and
towing-free practicality without a combustion engine. Families of four fit
comfortably; the boot swallows a week's luggage. It is less suited to anyone who
regularly drives more than 400 km in a single stint in winter, or who has no
predictable place to charge overnight.

## What it is not

It is not a performance SUV, and it is not the cheapest way into an electric SUV.
Both of those are deliberate — the car is tuned for quiet, unremarkable competence.
