# iX3 range — WLTP against the real world

## What WLTP measures

WLTP is a laboratory cycle run at a controlled temperature with no passengers, no
luggage and no headwind. It is useful for comparing two cars against each other and
close to useless for predicting the range of any single journey.

## What drivers actually see

On mixed driving — a mix of town, B-road and some motorway — most owners report
landing meaningfully below the official figure. The gap widens the faster you go,
because aerodynamic drag rises with the square of speed. A steady 130 km/h costs far
more per kilometre than a steady 100 km/h.

## What moves the number most

### Speed
The single largest factor on any long trip. Dropping a motorway cruise by 10 km/h
recovers more range than almost anything else you can do.

### Load and terrain
A full car with roof bars in hilly country will use noticeably more than an empty one
on the flat. Regeneration returns some of what a climb costs, but never all of it.

### Tyres and pressure
Under-inflated tyres are a quiet, permanent range tax. Check them monthly.

## Planning a long trip

Plan around the usable middle of the battery rather than its extremes. Charging from
10% to 80% is quick; the last 20% is slow enough that two shorter stops usually beat
one long one.
