# iX3 charging — AC at home, DC on the road

## AC charging at home

A domestic wallbox is how the great majority of charging happens. Plugged in
overnight, the car starts every morning at whatever state of charge you asked for.
This is the part that changes ownership most: for daily driving you effectively stop
visiting fuel stations.

A three-pin domestic socket works but is slow enough to be a fallback rather than a
plan.

## DC rapid charging

On a rapid charger the car pulls hardest when the battery is cool and fairly empty,
then tapers as it fills. That taper is chemistry, not a limitation of the charger.

### Why 10 to 80 percent is the useful window

Charging speed falls away sharply above roughly 80%. On a journey, two stops from 10%
to 80% almost always beat one stop from 10% to 100%.

### Preconditioning

If the car knows it is heading to a rapid charger, it warms the battery on the way so
it can accept a higher rate on arrival. Navigating to the charger, rather than simply
driving to it, is what triggers this.

## Charging etiquette

Move off the rapid charger once you are done. The taper means the last few percent
takes as long as the first half and blocks the bay for someone who needs it more.
